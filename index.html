Who we are

Vamos Falar, by Associação Into Change

Vamos Falar is the speaking-club programme of Associação Into Change, a non-profit association helping newcomers find their voice and belong in Portugal. Speaking clubs are our heart, because confidence comes from conversation.

FAQ

Good questions before you commit

I am a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. The First Words circle starts from zero and keeps things simple and friendly.

How is this different from an app or a regular class?

You speak in every session, in small groups, about situations from your actual life, with a host who guides you.

Can I try before I pay?

Yes. Your first class is free, so you can meet your host and see if it works for you.

Which Portuguese will I learn?

European Portuguese, which is also what the CIPLE tests.

In person or online?

Both. In-person clubs by neighbourhood, plus live online circles.

Where does the money go?

Part of every paid seat funds a free seat for a family who cannot afford one. We will publish how funds are used.

Can I get a free or reduced seat?

Yes. Apply with a short form and no paperwork.

Founding members

Vamos falar. Let's talk.

Founding members get first choice of club times, their launch rate locked in, and a free seat gifted in their name. Seats per club are limited.
